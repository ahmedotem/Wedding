Our Story

Neither of us is a yinzer, but Pittsburgh pulled us both in anyway. It pulled Briana down from upstate New York. It pulled Ahmed in from Washington, DC, a long way from where his journey began in Egypt. Two people who grew up more than 5,000 miles apart, yet so alike in all the ways that matter.

Once we were in the same city, our phones closed the last gap, as they do in so many modern love stories. It started with Briana’s quirky baby tooth, which Ahmed found adorable (he still does). We both swiped right, and we instinctively knew.

From there, we grew closer one message at a time. The more we talked, the more we had in common: a soft spot for pets, putting family and friends first, a curiosity to explore the world, and a simple outlook on life. Soon, those messages were the best part of our days.

When we finally met at Barcelona Wine Bar, the nerves faded fast. We talked for hours, like we’d known each other for years. By the end of the night, we’d learned two very important things: Briana hates olives, and Ahmed doesn’t own a camel.

There was never one big moment when we knew. Instead, there were a hundred small ones: cooking together on Sundays, starting a new show, pitching in for each other, kind words after a tough day, and a new pair of shoes showing up just when they were needed. With every one of them, our love kept growing.

So when Ahmed was ready to ask the big question, he knew exactly where to go. On April 8, 2026, on the pretense of celebrating an anniversary that had actually passed months earlier, we went back to Barcelona, the very spot of our first date. This time, there was a ring and a very happy yes. (And no, there still weren’t any olives.)

That yes opened a new chapter in New Jersey, where the four of us, Briana, Ahmed, Koda, and Kiko, started building a home together.

Now, our story is bringing us to the shores of Cayuta Lake, where we’ll get to celebrate with the people who mean the most to us. From Egypt and New York to Pittsburgh and everywhere life has taken us since, it’s pretty special to think of all the paths that led us here.

We can’t wait to have you there for the next part of our story.

Koda and Kiko send their regards.
