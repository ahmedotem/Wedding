Our Story

Ahmed grew up in Egypt. Briana grew up in Rochester, New York. Two lives that started half a world apart somehow found their way to the same city, Pittsburgh, where one swipe on Hinge brought them together.

We clicked right away: a soft spot for pets, a love for the simple life, and, of all things, her baby tooth.

For weeks we got to know each other one message at a time, without ever meeting. When we finally sat down across from each other at Barcelona Wine Bar in downtown Pittsburgh, it didn’t feel like a first date. It felt like meeting someone we already knew by heart.

Now, from Egypt and Rochester by way of Pittsburgh, we can’t wait to bring everyone we love together on the shores of Cayuta Lake to begin our forever.
