Our Story

We started out more than 5,000 miles apart: Ahmed in Egypt, Briana in Rochester, New York. Somehow both of our roads led to Pittsburgh, and to one very lucky swipe on Hinge.

For Ahmed, it was the baby tooth. Briana’s smile in her profile picture still had one, and he thought it was the cutest thing he’d ever seen. (He still does.)

For three weeks, we got to know each other one message at a time. Briana was shy and a little nervous, and Ahmed was happy to be patient. Each conversation made the next one easier, and before long, our messages were the best part of every day.

When we finally met at Barcelona Wine Bar in downtown Pittsburgh, Ahmed got there first, a little early and a little nervous. By the end of the night, he had learned two very important things: Briana hates olives, and she loves cheese. Mostly, though, it didn’t feel like a first date. It felt like meeting someone we already knew by heart.

There was no single moment when we knew. We just knew, and our love kept growing a little more every day. When we moved to New Jersey, Briana packed up her life and came along. Along the way, our family grew by two: Koda and Kiko, our Shiba Inus, who now run the household.

In 2026, Ahmed took us back to where it all began. At Barcelona, the spot of our very first date, he asked Briana to marry him. (She said yes. And no, there were no olives.)

We’ve always loved the simple things: nature, quiet moments, and the people who matter most. That’s why we chose the shores of Cayuta Lake for an intimate wedding with the people we love. From Egypt and Rochester, by way of Pittsburgh, we can’t wait to celebrate with you as we begin our forever.Hi
