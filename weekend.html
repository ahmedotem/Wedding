Good to know

  • It’s Labor Day weekend: book restaurants, tastings and boat rides ahead.
  • The Gorge Trail is wet and slippery, so wear shoes with good grip.
  • Get to Watkins Glen State Park early (before 10 AM) to beat the crowds.
  • Cell service can be spotty between towns. Download the area in Google Maps before you go.
  • Wineries and breweries usually close in the early evening, so plan tastings for the afternoon.
  • Tasting? Pick a designated driver or book a wine tour.
