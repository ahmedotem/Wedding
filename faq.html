Common Questions

When is the wedding?

The celebration will take place on Saturday, September 4, 2027, at the Fontainebleau Inn in Alpine, New York. The ceremony begins at 3:30 PM.

Where is the Fontainebleau Inn?

2800 State Route 228, Alpine, NY 14805, on Cayuta Lake in the Finger Lakes, about 20 minutes from Watkins Glen. See Travel & Stay for directions and airports.

Where should we stay?

The Inn’s rooms are all booked, so we recommend an Airbnb nearby. Our picks are on the map on Travel & Stay, along with hotels by area. A hotel block is in progress.

How do we get around?

A car is the best way to get around the area. We’re also arranging a transportation block, with details coming soon.

What is the dress code?

Black Tie Optional. We would love to see you in your most elegant attire for this special day.

How do I RSVP?

Kindly confirm your attendance by June 1, 2027, using the RSVP form on our website. Start with your name as it appears on your invitation. If you're replying for more than one person, please send one response for each guest.

Are kids invited?

We love your little ones! To give everyone a relaxing night out, our celebration will be adults-only, apart from the children in our family and wedding party. We hope you’ll enjoy an evening off with us.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Our celebration is small, so we’ve saved a seat for each guest by name. If your invitation includes a guest, the RSVP form will ask about them. Any questions? Please contact us.

What food will be served?

Dinner is by Word of Mouth Catering. Please list any dietary needs on your RSVP so we can share them with the caterer.

What is there to do nearby?

Lots! Waterfalls, Seneca Lake, wineries, breweries and two charming towns. See Things To Do.
